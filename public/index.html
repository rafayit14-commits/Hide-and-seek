or join one
How it works

Everyone joins a room. When the host starts, hunters are picked at random. Runners get a head start to hide.

Every few minutes the hunters receive every runner's location. The zone shrinks on a timer, and the next zone is shown dashed on the map. A runner who stays outside the zone longer than the grace time is disqualified.

Hunters win if every runner is out. Runners win if anyone survives until the clock runs out.

Keep the app open with the screen on while you play, so your phone keeps sending your location.
